Editions

One system.
Six contexts.

Every community has its own version of the same silence. Each Unmasked edition speaks to a specific one.

The Shield taxonomy does not change between editions. Gaslighting is gaslighting in a marriage, in a workplace, in a church. What changes is the scenario language, the specific situations, relationships, and cultural pressures that make each tactic recognisable in that context.

Classic EditionAvailable now

Origins

For open community groups, NGO programmes, introductory corporate sessions, and any gathering where the doors are open to everyone. Origins is the entry point, the edition that works for any community encountering Unmasked for the first time. All 10 Shield tactics. All 100 scenario cards. No assumed context, which is itself a design decision.

Enquire about Origins
Marriage EditionIn development

To Love & To Hold

For married couples, pre-marital programmes, marriage enrichment organisations, and any community navigating the specific dynamics of long-term committed relationships. This edition addresses the tactics that normalise most deeply inside marriages, where the relationship history itself becomes a tool of leverage, and where cultural and religious expectations compound the silence.

Dating EditionIn development

What Are We

For young adults, unmarried couples, and communities navigating the complicated early terrain of modern relationships. This edition speaks to the dynamics that emerge when commitment is undefined, where the lack of formal status is itself used as a reason not to name harm.

Religion EditionIn development

This Can't Be God

For faith communities (churches, mosques, religious organisations) where cultural and religious authority is part of the everyday landscape of relationships. This edition does not position faith as the problem. It addresses the weaponisation of faith: the specific way spiritual language and religious authority are used to silence legitimate concerns.
