Get involved

Silence spreads
when no one names it.

There are four ways to work with Unmasked. Every one of them ends the same way: with more people who can name what is happening to them.

For organisations

Commission a programme

NGOs, corporates, faith communities, schools, and government institutions can commission an Unmasked programme for their community or cohort. Choose from three tiers, with full monitoring and evaluation reporting included.

Book a session or enquire
For facilitators

Train to facilitate Unmasked

Unmasked sessions are only ever run by trained facilitators working from the Moderator's Manual. If you work in community development, counselling, HR, or education, and you want to run sessions in your own community, tell us about your experience.

Apply to train as a facilitator
For partners & funders

Partner with Unmasked

We work with funders, advocacy bodies, and support organisations who want to extend the reach of the programme through funding, referral pathways, co-designed editions, or shared impact reporting.

Start a partnership conversation
For individuals

Carry the language forward

You do not need to run a programme to change something. Learning the ten tactics and using their names in your own conversations is advocacy. Join the community and we will send you the language, one tactic at a time.

Learn the ten tactics

Join the Unmasked community

We send advocacy content, tactic explainers, and session announcements by email and WhatsApp. No crisis support (for that, use our support directory). Just the language, arriving regularly, so you can keep using it.
