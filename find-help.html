Unmasked is an education programme, not a crisis service. These are organisations in Nigeria that provide direct support.

In immediate danger? Call 112 now.

The Exit button at the top of this page will close this site and send your browser elsewhere immediately. This page is also hidden from search engines.

  • Emergency services

    112 (toll-free, nationwide)

    If you or someone else is in immediate physical danger, call 112. This is the national emergency number and reaches police, ambulance, and fire services.

  • Police Gender Desk

    Available in most state commands

    Most state police commands operate a Gender Desk that handles domestic and sexual violence cases. Ask specifically for the Gender Desk when you attend a station.

  • Mirabel Centre (Lagos)

    0815 577 0000 | mirabelcentre.org

    Sexual assault referral centre based at Lagos State University Teaching Hospital, Ikeja. Free medical care, forensic services, and counselling.

  • Lagos State DSVA

    0800 0333 333 | 0813 796 0048 | dsva.lagosstate.gov.ng

    The Lagos State Domestic and Sexual Violence Agency provides legal support, shelter referral, and case management for survivors in Lagos. The 0800 line is toll-free.

  • WARIF

    0800 9210 0009 | warifng.org (24/7, toll-free)

    Women at Risk International Foundation runs a 24-hour sexual assault referral centre in Yaba, Lagos, with medical care and counselling.

  • Project Alert on Violence Against Women

    0818 009 1072 | projectalertnig.org

    Nationwide advocacy, legal aid, and shelter support for women experiencing violence.

  • FCT Sexual and Gender-Based Violence Response Team

    Reach through the FCT Police Command Gender Desk, or call 112

    Coordinated response, referral, and case support for survivors in Abuja and the Federal Capital Territory.

  • Mentally Aware Nigeria Initiative (MANI)

    0809 111 6264 | mentallyaware.org

    Mental health support and crisis counselling by phone and WhatsApp, nationwide.

This directory is provided for information only. Unmasked does not operate these services and cannot guarantee availability or response times. If a number does not connect, call 112 or attend the nearest police Gender Desk or teaching hospital.
