The Shield: tactics of Normalised Relation AbuseTen tactics.
Named plainly.

These are the categories of Normalised Relation Abuse at the heart of every Unmasked session. Read them. Learn the language. Recognise it when you see it.

Unmasked is an education programme, not a crisis service. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 112. Find support organisations →

Each tactic below has its own page: a plain-language definition, examples of what it sounds like in real conversation, and the Grey card test, the question that separates what is abuse from what only looks like it.

    Gaslighting
