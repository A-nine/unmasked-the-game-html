Privacy & safety

Your information,
handled carefully.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us: the details you enter into an enquiry form or the advocacy sign-up (name, phone number, email address, state, and the description of your organisation or need). We do not ask you to disclose personal experiences of abuse anywhere on this site, and you should not enter them into any form here.

How we use it

Enquiry details are used to respond to you and prepare a proposal. Advocacy sign-up details are used to send you tactic explainers, advocacy content, and session announcements by email and WhatsApp. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Participant data

Monitoring and evaluation forms completed during Unmasked sessions are anonymous. They capture awareness shift and session quality, never identifying details or disclosures. Facilitators are bound by the confidentiality provisions of the Moderator's Manual.

Safety on this site

A Quick Exit button appears on every page. It clears this site from your current session and redirects your browser away immediately. Our support directory page is excluded from search engine indexing so that visiting it leaves less of a trace.

Unmasked is an education programme, not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call 112.

Removing your details

Email hello@unmasked.ng at any time and we will delete your information from our records and unsubscribe you from all communication.
